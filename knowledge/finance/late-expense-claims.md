An expense claim is considered late if submitted more than 30 days after the date the expense was incurred (not the report-submission date).

## The 30-90 Day Window

Claims submitted between 30 and 90 days after the expense date require manager approval, regardless of the amount. Standard self-approval limits do not apply to late claims.

To submit a claim within this window, the employee must provide a justification for the delay in the portal (e.g., "traveling," "receipt lost and reissued"). This field cannot be left blank. There is no preset list of acceptable reasons; the manager evaluates both the business expense and the reason for the delay.

## Hard Cutoff: 90 Days

Any claim submitted past 90 days from the expense date is permanently ineligible for reimbursement.

This is a firm limit with no exceptions. The portal automatically flags these claims as closed, and they cannot be approved by anyone. The 90-day rule governs exclusively; the fiscal year-end does not introduce a separate cutoff.
