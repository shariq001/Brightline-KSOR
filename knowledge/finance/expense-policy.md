This policy covers reasonable business expenses incurred by Brightline employees while performing company duties, including travel, client meetings, and remote-work equipment. It applies to all full-time employees.

## Submitting an Expense

Employees should submit expense reports through the company expense portal with a valid receipt attached. Reports should be submitted promptly after the expense is incurred.

## Approval Limits

| Category | Employee Self-Approve Up To | Requires Manager Approval Above |
|---|---|---|
| Meals (per day) | $40 | $40 |
| Local transport | $25 | $25 |
| Client entertainment | $100 | $100 |
| Travel (flights, hotels) | Manager approval always required | — |
| Software / subscriptions | $50/month | $50/month |

## Reimbursement Timing

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next available payroll cycle following approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The company does not reimburse personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, or expenses that are not reasonable and necessary for business purposes.

## International Travel

Employees travelling internationally for business should use good judgment regarding local costs and currency conversion.

## Disputed or Rejected Expenses

If an expense is rejected, the employee may raise the matter with their manager.
