# Brightline Return & Refund Policy

At Brightline, we want you to be absolutely thrilled with the digital products and services we provide. We know things don't always go as planned, so we have created a clear and fair refund policy. 

Below are the conditions under which refunds and cancellations are handled for our various services.

---

## 1. SaaS Subscriptions (Monthly & Annual Plans)

When you subscribe to one of our self-serve platforms (like the Starter or Professional tier), here is how refunds work:

- **14-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: If you sign up for a new SaaS subscription and realize it isn't the right fit, simply let us know within the first 14 days. We will issue a 100% full refund, no questions asked. 
- **Cancellations**: You are free to cancel your subscription at any time right from your dashboard. Once you cancel, your account will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle. 
- **Prorated Refunds**: Please note that we do not offer partial or prorated refunds if you cancel in the middle of a billing cycle (e.g., cancelling two weeks into a monthly plan).

---

## 2. Custom Development & AaaS (Agents as a Service)

Custom projects—such as building a new website or setting up a dedicated AI RAG pipeline—require significant time and engineering resources. Because of this, our refund structure works a bit differently:

- **Milestone-Based Billing**: We bill for custom work in stages (milestones) defined in a Statement of Work (SOW) that we both agree on before starting.
- **Refund Eligibility**: If Brightline fails to deliver a specific milestone as promised in the SOW, you are completely eligible to request a partial or full refund for that specific milestone. We evaluate these requests fairly on a case-by-case basis.
- **Completed Work**: Once a milestone is successfully delivered to you and you have signed off on it, no refunds can be provided for that completed work.

---

## 3. How to Request a Refund

Requesting a refund is simple and hassle-free:
1. Send an email to `billing@brightline.com`.
2. Include your account name, the email associated with your account, and a brief reason for the request (so we can learn and improve!).
3. Our friendly finance team will review your request and get back to you within **2 to 3 business days**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Enterprise Contracts">
If you are an Enterprise client, your refund and cancellation terms are governed strictly by the custom Master Services Agreement (MSA) signed during onboarding. Please refer to your MSA for specific details.
</Callout>
