# Brightline Pricing Policy

At Brightline, we believe that pricing should be transparent, easy to understand, and directly tied to the value we provide your business. We offer flexible pricing models tailored to the scale of your operations, whether you need a turnkey SaaS subscription, a custom website, or advanced AI agents.

## 2. Custom Web Development & RAG Solutions

If you need us to build a completely custom digital product from scratch—such as a brand-new website or a bespoke Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) database—we use a **Milestone-Based** pricing model. This ensures you only pay as we deliver concrete results.

### Phase 1: Discovery & Architecture
**Estimated Cost: $2,500 - $5,000 (One-time fee)**
Before we write any code, we figure out exactly what you need. We deliver technical blueprints, UI/UX design wireframes, and design the structure of your RAG vector database. 

### Phase 2: Development Iterations (Milestones)
**Estimated Cost: $10,000 - $25,000+ per milestone**
We break the project down into functional pieces (milestones). For example, Milestone 1 might be deploying the frontend website, and Milestone 2 might be integrating your AI Agent Swarm. You are billed as each milestone is successfully delivered and approved by you.

### Phase 3: Maintenance & Hosting
**Starting at: $500 / month**
Once your custom product is live, we keep it running smoothly. This covers server hosting costs, continuous security updates, and regular maintenance of your vector database to ensure your AI stays smart.
