This page summarizes how Brightline Software Inc protects customer data and
maintains platform reliability.

## Certifications & Audits

- SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA on request.
- Annual third-party penetration testing; summary results available to
  Enterprise customers on request.

## Infrastructure

- Hosted on redundant, geographically distributed cloud infrastructure.
- 99.9% uptime target for the core platform, with historical uptime published
  on our public status page.

## Data Protection

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
- Role-based access controls and audit logging for internal systems that
  touch customer data.
- For details on what data is collected and retention timelines, see
  `data-privacy-policy.md`.

## Incident Disclosure

In the event of a security incident affecting customer data, Brightline will
notify affected customers in accordance with applicable law and contractual
commitments. Status updates during active incidents are posted to our public
status page.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Security researchers can report suspected vulnerabilities to our security
team through the contact listed on our public security page. We do not
publish internal escalation contacts or incident response procedures, which
are handled by internal teams.
