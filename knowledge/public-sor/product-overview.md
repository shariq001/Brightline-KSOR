Brightline is a project-management SaaS platform built by Brightline Software Inc. It helps teams plan work, track progress, and collaborate across departments from a single workspace.

## What Brightline Does

Brightline centralizes project planning, task execution, and reporting so teams don't have to stitch together spreadsheets, chat threads, and separate tools. It's used by teams ranging from five-person startups to multi-thousand-person enterprises.

## Core Features

- **Task Boards** — Kanban-style boards with custom workflows, swimlanes, and WIP limits.
- **Timelines & Gantt Views** — Visual scheduling with dependency tracking and critical-path highlighting.
- **Resource Management** — Workload views to see who's over- or under-allocated across projects.
- **Reporting & Dashboards** — Configurable dashboards for burndown, velocity, and portfolio-level status.
- **Integrations** — Native integrations with Slack, GitHub, and Google Calendar; open REST API for custom integrations.
- **Mobile Apps** — iOS and Android apps for on-the-go updates and approvals.
- **Automation Rules** — No-code "if this, then that" rules for recurring task actions (e.g., auto-assign, auto-notify).

## Who It's For

Brightline is designed for cross-functional teams — engineering, marketing, operations, and professional services — that need shared visibility into project status without heavyweight project-management overhead.
