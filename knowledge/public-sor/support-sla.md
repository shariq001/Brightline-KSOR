Response-time commitments vary by plan tier. All times refer to first response, not resolution time.

| Plan       | Channels               | Response Time            | Hours                     |
|------------|-------------------------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| Starter    | Community forum, email  | 48 hours                  | Business hours (Mon–Fri)   |
| Team       | Email, in-app chat      | 24 hours                  | Business hours (Mon–Fri)   |
| Business   | Email, chat, phone      | 8 hours                   | Extended hours (Mon–Sat)   |
| Enterprise | Email, chat, phone, dedicated CSM | 1 hour for critical issues; 4 hours for standard issues | 24/7 |

## Definitions

- **Critical issue**: Production outage or data-loss risk affecting the customer's ability to use Brightline.
- **Standard issue**: Functional bugs or questions that do not block core usage.

## Escalation

Customers can request escalation of an open ticket through their support contact or, for Enterprise customers, directly through their dedicated CSM. Internal escalation procedures are documented separately for Brightline staff.
