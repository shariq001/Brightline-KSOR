# Brightline Support, SLA & Contract Policy

When you partner with Brightline, you aren't just buying software; you are getting a dedicated team to back it up. This document explains how quickly we promise to respond to your issues and outlines our standard contract terms.

---

## 1. Guaranteed Response Times

We measure our support by **First Response Time**—which is how quickly a human engineer or customer success manager will reply to your ticket after you submit it. The speed depends on your subscription tier.

| Plan / Service Tier | How to Contact Us | Guaranteed First Response | Support Hours |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starter SaaS** | Email, Ticket Portal | Within 48 hours | Business hours (Mon-Fri) |
| **Professional SaaS** | Email, Ticket Portal, Live Chat | Within 12 hours | Extended hours (Mon-Sat) |
| **Enterprise SaaS / AaaS** | Email, Phone, Dedicated CSM | 1 hour (Critical) <br/> 4 hours (Standard) | 24/7/365 |
| **Custom Web Development** | Shared Slack Channel, Email | Within 8 hours (During Active Project) | Business hours (Mon-Fri) |

### What is a "Critical" Issue?
For Enterprise clients, a "Critical" issue means your platform is completely down or severely unusable (e.g., users cannot log in, payments cannot process). "Standard" issues cover bugs, questions, or minor feature breaks.

---

## 2. Uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA)

We know that downtime costs you money. That is why Brightline guarantees **99.9% uptime** for all SaaS platforms and managed Agentic infrastructure for our **Enterprise clients**. 

**What happens if we miss the mark?**
- If uptime falls below 99.9% in a single month, we automatically credit your account for 10% of that month's bill.
- If it falls below 99.0%, we credit you 30% of that month's bill.

*Please note: Routine scheduled maintenance (which we will warn you about at least 48 hours in advance) does not count as downtime.*

---

## 3. Simple Contract Policies

We don't like confusing legal jargon any more than you do. Here are our standard terms:

- **SaaS Subscriptions**: You are billed month-to-month or annually. There is absolutely no long-term lock-in unless you request a custom Master Services Agreement (MSA).
- **Custom Projects (Agentic & RAG)**: We bill per project milestone. We will both sign a clear Statement of Work (SOW) so you know exactly what is being built before any engineering begins.
- **Privacy First**: All custom development and enterprise contracts automatically include a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Your secrets are safe with us.
