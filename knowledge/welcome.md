### RAG and MCP Server Connectivity

Brightline pioneers the use of **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**. Our private vector databases allow autonomous AI agents to search securely through isolated silos of company data. 

Furthermore, this very KSOR is integrated with a **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server**. This means that autonomous AI agents can connect directly to this repository to retrieve verified, cited answers for you. If a piece of information is not contained within this corpus, our connected AI agents are instructed to safely **abstain** from answering rather than making up information.
