Customers can return an item within 30 days of purchase provided they have a receipt[^prompt-2026-09-14]. Refunds land within 5 working days[^prompt-2026-09-14].

Items must be unused to qualify for a return under the standard window[^prompt-2026-09-14]. However, faulty goods are exempt from this window and can be returned even if used or outside the standard timeframe[^prompt-2026-09-14].
