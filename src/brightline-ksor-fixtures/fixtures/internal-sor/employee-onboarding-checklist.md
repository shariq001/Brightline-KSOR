# Employee Onboarding Checklist (Internal)

Standard checklist for a new Brightline employee's first week.

## Before Day 1 (People Ops + IT)

- Provision company email and SSO account.
- Order laptop and ship to employee (or prep for office pickup).
- Add to relevant Slack channels for their team.
- Assign an onboarding buddy from within their team.

## Day 1

- IT: laptop setup session, MFA enrollment, password manager setup.
- People Ops: benefits overview, org chart walkthrough, review of employee
  handbook.
- Manager: 1:1 to review 30/60/90-day expectations.

## Week 1

- Complete mandatory security awareness training (required before any
  production system access is granted).
- Complete role-specific system access requests (e.g., Zendesk for Support,
  Salesforce for Sales, AWS/GitHub for Engineering) via the access request
  form; access is granted based on role per `access-control.yaml`-equivalent
  internal IT policy.
- Shadow a buddy on at least one real task or ticket.

## Notes

- Access to restricted internal documents (e.g., unreleased roadmap
  materials) is not part of standard onboarding and requires manager
  sign-off separately.
