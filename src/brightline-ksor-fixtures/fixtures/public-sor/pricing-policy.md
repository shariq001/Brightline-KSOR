# Brightline Pricing Policy

Brightline is offered in four plan tiers, billed per user per month (annual billing available at a discount).

## Starter — $9/user/month

- Task boards and basic timelines
- Up to 3 projects
- Slack integration
- Community support (see `support-sla.md`)

## Team — $19/user/month

- Everything in Starter
- Unlimited projects
- Gantt views with dependency tracking
- GitHub and Google Calendar integrations
- Standard email and chat support

## Business — $35/user/month

- Everything in Team
- Resource management and workload views
- Advanced reporting and custom dashboards
- Automation rules
- Priority support with phone access

## Enterprise — Custom Pricing

- Everything in Business
- Single sign-on (SSO) and SCIM provisioning
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager
- Custom contract terms and invoicing
- Enterprise-grade support commitments (see `support-sla.md`)

## Notes

- All paid plans include a 14-day free trial.
- Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes.
- Discounts outside of published pricing are not authorized publicly; contact sales for enterprise quotes.
